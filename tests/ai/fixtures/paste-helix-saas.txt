# Company

Helix is a vertical SaaS for mid-market commercial general contractors (GCs). We replace the spreadsheet-and-email mess that GCs use to track subcontractor compliance docs (insurance certs, W-9s, safety training records, lien waivers) with a single dashboard that auto-chases the subs and flags expired docs before they become a jobsite shutdown risk.

# Product

Core features:
- Sub directory with compliance status per sub (insurance, W-9, safety, lien waivers)
- Auto-chase: configurable email + SMS reminders to subs when their docs are within 30 / 14 / 7 days of expiring
- Field-mobile sub-onboarding (the differentiator — a sub can complete a full document upload + verification in 6 minutes on their phone in the field)
- Bilingual sub portal (Spanish-language; we'll add Portuguese before Series A)
- Integrations with Procore and Sage 300 CRE; QuickBooks Enterprise integration shipping in May
- Lien-waiver generation + e-signature (built on top of Anvil for the templating, DocuSign for the signing — switching to internal e-sign Q3)

The bilingual sub portal is our moat against Procore. ~40% of the field workforce on commercial construction jobsites in the US Southwest is Spanish-first. Procore's portal is English-only and would require a top-down product reorg to internationalize. We shipped Spanish in 4 weeks because we built it that way from day one.

# Why now

Three macro forces:

1. **Subcontractor default risk is rising.** Construction subcontractor bankruptcies hit a 10-year high in 2024 (per Levelset's data). When a sub defaults, the GC is on the hook for unpaid labor + materials liens. The fastest way to avoid this is rigorous up-front and ongoing compliance verification — exactly what we do.
2. **OSHA enforcement is tightening.** The 2024 OSHA budget added 200 inspectors and shifted enforcement priorities toward construction. Citations for missing safety-training documentation rose 31% YoY in 2024. The cost of a jobsite shutdown for a documentation gap is now $40–80k per day for mid-market GCs.
3. **The Spanish-speaking sub workforce is the demographic reality.** Per BLS, ~36% of construction laborers in the US are Hispanic; in the Southwest it's over 50%. Software that doesn't meet them in their language is leaving compliance gaps on every job.

# Pricing

Per-project + per-sub + platform fee model:
- $1,200/mo platform fee per GC (covers up to 5 active projects)
- $6/sub/month for each tracked sub above the included 50 subs
- $400/mo per additional active project above 5
- Average ACV across our 19 paying accounts: $26k. Top decile: $58k. The big Denver GC is $94k ARR.

Pricing is the lever I'm most uncertain about. Wendy Coffey is pushing me to move to a pure platform-fee model ($2,400/mo flat, includes everything) because the per-sub line item creates friction in the procurement conversation. I think she's right; we're A/B testing it on new pilots in May.
