==

The problem (this is the part I keep getting wrong when I describe it on demo days, help me get it tight)

The 12 million working-class Mexicans living in the US send roughly $63B/year back to family in Mexico. The current options are bad in three ways at once:

1. Western Union / MoneyGram: 5–7% effective fee once you net out the FX spread. Trust is high (mom knows the brand) but the cost compounds — a $400 transfer every two weeks for a year is ~$2,500 of fees burned.
2. App-based remitters (Remitly, Wise): better pricing, but they all require the sender to maintain a checking account at a US bank. Roughly 22% of our target users are underbanked or unbanked entirely. They literally cannot use Remitly without first getting a Chime card, which is its own painful onboarding cliff.
3. Crypto rails (USDC + a local off-ramp): cheap, but the UX is unusable for someone who doesn't already speak Web3. We tested this — 100% of the 14 users we ran through a USDC-based flow gave up before completing a transfer.

What everyone misses: this isn't really a remittance problem. It's a "where do I store the money between paychecks" problem. The sender wants to keep a dollar balance in a US-regulated account, earn a little yield, and send a chunk home on the 1st and 15th. Remittance is a feature of the savings account, not the other way around.

==

What we're building

Acme is a US-dollar savings account (sponsored by Bond Bank, our partner BIN sponsor — LOI signed March 2025, MSA in legal review) for non-US-citizen workers. Federally insured up to FDIC limits via the sponsor bank. The deposit account earns 4.1% APY (passed through from the sponsor's money-market vehicle).

Layered on top:
- Free transfers to any CLABE in Mexico, settled same-day via SPEI. No fees, mid-market FX rate, capped at $2,500 per transfer / $10k per month per user.
- Bi-weekly "auto-send" — user sets a recurring transfer of $X to their mom's account, debited automatically the day after their paycheck hits.
- Bilingual app (Spanish-first; English toggle). Voice walkthrough for first-time users — we tested this with my aunt in Phoenix and it cut onboarding time from 14 minutes to 4.

The big unlock is the auto-send. Everyone else makes you remember to send. We make sending the default and storing the harder action.

==

Traction (as of today — the numbers we'd put in front of an investor)

- Closed beta opened February 2025. 1,140 users onboarded by mid-April.
- 92% complete KYC in <5 minutes (the underbanked segment hit 87%, which is the bar I care about).
- MRR sitting at $24,000 at end of April. That's the interchange + FX spread + a sponsorship rev-share from Bond Bank. Mostly interchange right now — the FX volume is the lever.
- Total US-dollar deposits in the program: $1.94M. Average balance per active user: ~$1,700. Higher than I expected (I'd modeled $900); these users are more savings-oriented than the narrative gives them credit for.
- We've moved £18,000 of pilot volume through a parallel UK-Mexico corridor (a side experiment Diego ran with three London-based domestic workers introduced via a NGO partner). Useful signal but not strategic — we'll shut it down before seed.
- Growth has been ~38% MoM for the last 3 months. Mostly word-of-mouth out of two Catholic parishes in Phoenix and one in Houston.
- Burn: $42k/month. Cash on hand: $310k (after the $400k pre-seed SAFE we closed in January led by an angel syndicate out of Houston). Runway: ~7 months at current burn, which is why we're starting the priced seed conversation now.

==

Why now

- Bond Bank changed their sponsorship terms in Q4 2024 — they now underwrite ITIN-only customers, which historically every BIN sponsor refused. That single underwriting policy change opens the underbanked segment to a regulated fintech for the first time.
- SPEI added 24/7 settlement in mid-2024 — so a US-side debit at 11pm Friday actually lands in mom's account by Saturday morning. Pre-2024 this was a Monday at the earliest, and the UX was destroyed by that delay.
- Generative-AI customer support (we use Anthropic) made the cost of bilingual, voice-first onboarding tractable for a 2-person team. Two years ago we'd have needed a 6-person CX team to ship the same UX.

Where we are on the raise

We're raising a $2.5M seed at a $14M post. Lead is unconfirmed — three funds are in diligence (Forerunner, Maveron, and a Mexico-focused fund I'm not naming until we have a term sheet). The angel syndicate from January is rolling their pro-rata.

Use of funds: extend runway to 18 months, hire 1 senior eng + 1 compliance lead + 1 growth marketer, and run the auto-send experiment to 10,000 active users.

I want to be clear with investors that this is not a regulated bank — we're a fintech sitting on top of Bond Bank's charter. The deposit account is FDIC-insured because Bond Bank holds the relationship. We've never told a user "Acme is a bank" and the compliance team makes us shout this everywhere — the marketing copy says "powered by Bond Bank, Member FDIC" in 11-point type on every screen.

One more compliance note: the 4.1% APY is a passed-through deposit rate from Bond Bank's money-market sweep, not a yield product. The marketing copy avoids the word "yield" entirely and never frames the account as a return-generating savings vehicle.
