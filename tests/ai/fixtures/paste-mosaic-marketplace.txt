## What Mosaic is

Mosaic is a vetted two-sided marketplace where small independent furniture makers (the "Makers" — usually 1–3 person woodworking, ceramics, or metal shops) sell directly to interior designers and small architecture studios (the "Designers"). We're not a Etsy. Etsy is for consumer end-buyers. We're for the trade — designers who specify a credenza for a client and need it built to spec, with a real lead time, a real trade discount, and a real account manager.

The category we replace: the "designer trade program" that big brands (Roche Bobois, BDDW, etc.) run, where designers get 30–40% off, plus the long tail of furniture-maker discovery that designers do today via Instagram DMs and Pinterest. We give them one searchable, vetted marketplace with the same trade discount baked in, real lead-time visibility, and a payments stack that doesn't make the Maker float production costs against a 90-day net.

Founded January 2024. Incorporated as Mosaic Trade, Inc. in Delaware (C-Corp; we did the Delaware flip from the original NY LLC in July 2024 when our first institutional check landed). HQ is Brooklyn, two-person operations team in a coworking space in Greenpoint.

## Numbers

Marketplace metrics (as of mid-April):

- 142 vetted Makers on the platform (~280 in the queue, 38% acceptance rate from our vetting process)
- 491 designers signed up; 312 of them have placed at least one order

- We're not flat — GMV is growing 41% QoQ
- Our average Maker takes ~6 weeks to deliver, median is 5 weeks. We've had two Makers fail to deliver (1.4% of orders) and we ate the refund both times — that's the cost of vetting failures, and it's been worth it for the trust signal with designers
- Repeat rate: 38% of designers who placed a Q4 order placed another in Q1. That's the number I'm most proud of. It means the matching is working

A note on the GMV calculation: the $580k Q1 figure includes a single $90k commercial-restaurant fit-out (a designer in Chicago specified 12 custom tables and 48 chairs from one of our Makers for a new restaurant). That's a tail outlier. If I strip it out, Q1 GMV was $490k. I have been showing investors the $580k figure but explicitly disclosing the outlier in the same line of the deck.

## Why now

Three things converged:

1. Designer trade is shifting from brick-and-mortar showrooms to digital-first discovery. The 1stDibs IPO was the inflection — designers proved they will close 5-figure transactions online if the vetting + lead-time signals are right. We are not a 1stDibs (they're a luxury-vintage marketplace; we're new-make custom). But they cracked the trust problem and we're inheriting that tailwind.
2. Independent makers are getting wrecked by post-pandemic real-estate cost increases. The marginal Maker in Brooklyn has had their workshop rent go up 40% since 2021. They need higher-margin sales channels (direct-to-trade) to survive. We're a survival tool for them. That motivates an unusually high quality of supply — Makers will jump through our vetting hoops because we materially change their P&L.
3. The big brands that dominate trade today (Roche Bobois, B&B Italia, Kelly Wearstler's licensing program at Visual Comfort) have 16–24 week lead times that the new generation of designers — who run smaller, faster studios — can't tolerate. Our average lead time is 6 weeks. That's not us being scrappy; it's structural — small Makers can mobilize faster than big factories.

## Pricing for the Makers

Makers pay nothing to list. We take 14% of GMV at transaction. We pay Makers via a hold-and-release model — 50% on order acceptance, 50% on customer-confirmed delivery. We float the working capital between the two payment events (financed via a $250k Mercury working-capital facility, drawn down monthly).

## Pricing for the Designers

Designers pay $0 to use the marketplace. They get a 30% trade discount baked into the listed price (the Maker prices their product at retail, we surface the trade-discounted price to verified designers). Verification is a $50 one-time fee that gets refunded against their first $500 of orders (designed to eat fake-account spam).

## Differentiators / moat

1. **Maker vetting funnel.** 142 accepted out of ~840 applications since launch. We have a 6-criteria rubric (portfolio quality, production capacity, financial stability, communication responsiveness, prior trade experience, photo quality of listings). The bar is steep; this is the moat.
2. **Lead-time honesty.** Every Maker commits to a lead time on every order. We track delivery accuracy. Makers who miss twice are paused; three strikes = removed. Our average delivery hits within 4 days of the committed lead time, which is unheard of in custom furniture.
3. **Direct relationships, no rebadging.** The Maker is named on every order. The designer can chat directly with the Maker through our messaging. No anonymous middleman. This is the inverse of what 1stDibs does (they obfuscate the seller).
4. **Hassan's matching stack.** The search and ranking is genuinely better than competitors — designers find a vetted Maker they'd actually work with in 2 searches instead of 14.
