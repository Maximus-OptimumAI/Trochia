NfX's pipeline guidance describes stage-coded tracking of investor conversations (sourced, first-meeting, diligence, term-sheet, closed/lost) and a weekly cadence of follow-ups against each stage. Founders use the framework to keep a raise running like a sales pipeline rather than a stream of one-off threads.
