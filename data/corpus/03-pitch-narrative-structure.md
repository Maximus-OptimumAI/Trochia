Paul Graham's founder essays outline the narrative arc seed pitches tend to follow — problem framing, founder insight, the solution, traction signal, and the ask — and stress concrete language over abstraction. Founders use the arc as a structural check when sequencing slides or rehearsing the verbal pitch.
